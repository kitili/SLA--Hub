# Introduction

This is the third book in a series of three books for Reading for primary schools. The book is prepared according to the 2016 Basic Education Syllabus for Standard II issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. The book consists of fourteen units. Each unit contains activities for you to develop the reading skills. You are encouraged to do all activities in order to develop the intended competence.

## The sunny day

One day, Amina and her brother were walking along the road after school. The sun was in the sky. The day was very hot. The heavy bags loaded with books became heavier.
