# Our culture

> I recognise the traditional houses of Tanzania.

## Traditional houses
A house is a building where people live in. Traditional houses are the type of houses that are used by the Tanzanian community as their home.

## Types of traditional houses
Tanzania has different types of traditional houses such as msonge, banda and tembe.

### Msonge house
Msonge house is a type of house built with a pointed top. They are built by using poles, banana leaves, tree bark, mud, plant branches or tough grass. Some Tanzanian communities that use these houses include the Wahaya, Wasafwa, wafipa, Wachaga, Waha and Wanyambo.

I recognise and appreciate the beliefs of other people.

People go to pray so that they become good and kind with acceptable ethics. There are different religions like Islam, Christianity and Hinduism in our community. ## The ball-search game

The ball-searching game involves two teams. The team members find a space to pass the ball to a teammate behind the opposing team. The player behind the opposing team catches the ball to score points for their team. This game can be played in two ways by using hands and feet.

### How to play

**Part one (by using hands):**

1. Prepare your body by running slowly for 2 to 3 minutes, then stretch your body parts;
2. Choose a flat area, then to draw the **H** shape as shown in the illustration;

[diagram: Pitch layout showing five vertical zones: Searching zone, Defending zone, Free zone, Defending zone, and Searching zone.]

How to divide the pitch for a ball-searching game

--- PAGE 65 ---

<!--folio:59-->

3. Form two teams of six players each;
4. Five players will stay inside the defending zone, and one will play behind the opposing team in the search zone;
5. Team members are not allowed to enter the free zone and search zone. Only the searcher is allowed to stay in the search zone outside the team's zone;
6. Each team will pass the ball with hands to find a space to pass it to their searcher to score points;
7. Each team has the responsibility to prevent the opposing team from passing the ball through their zone; and
8. If the searcher catches the ball, they will return to the team, and the one who passed the ball through will become the searcher.

[diagram: Children playing the ball-searching game on a green-colored court split into zones]

Ball-searching game by using hands

The winning team is the one that all players have become searchers.

**Part two (by using feet):**

1. Prepare your body by running slowly for 2 to 3 minutes, then stretch your body;
2. Use a flat area to draw the **H** shape as shown in the illustration in part one of the game;
3. Form two teams of six players each;
4. Five players for each team will stay inside the defending zone, and one will play behind the opposing team in the search zone;
5. Team members are not allowed to enter the free zone or search zone. Only the searcher is allowed to stay in the search zone outside the team's zone;
6. Each team will pass and dribble the ball with feet to find a space to pass it to their searcher to score points;
7. Each team also has the responsibility to prevent the opposing team from passing the ball through into their zone; and
8. If the searcher receives the ball without involving hands, they will return to the team, and the one who passed the ball will become the searcher.
