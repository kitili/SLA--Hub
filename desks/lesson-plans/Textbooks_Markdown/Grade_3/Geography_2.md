# Introduction

This textbook, Geography and the Environment for Primary Schools, is written on the basis of the Geography and the Environment syllabus for Primary school Education Standards III-VI, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology in 2023. Some of the themes in this book have been taken from the previous book for Social Studies standards III and IV published in 2018 in accordance with the 2016 syllabus.

The book consists of four (4) chapters, namely The concepts of Geography and the Environment, Earth's relief, Environmental conservation and Environmental degradation. By learning the content of this textbook, you will acquire knowledge, skills and attitudes necessary for environmental conservation and for appreciating the value of our natural resources.

The content of each chapter is presented in the form of text, activities, diagrams, exercises and pictures to enable you to build the intended competencies. Each chapter has adequate exercises to test your understanding of the specific themes.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-library, at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz

# Chapter One

## The concepts of Geography and the Environment

### Introduction

Human beings need to understand the relationship between Geography and the Environment so that they know the best ways to interact with their surroundings. Geography enables human beings to understand the surrounding environment and to conserve and use it sustainably. In this chapter, you will learn about the meanings, interaction and importance of learning Geography and the Environment. The competencies to be developed will enable you to value and care for the environment.

### Think

[diagram: A stick figure thinking]

Things that surround human beings

### Meaning of Geography

Geography is a subject that deals with the study of human beings and their environment. Geography is concerned with the earth, the atmosphere and things that exist on the earth's surface. Geography is also concerned with the distribution of things and their interaction with human beings. The earth's surface comprises the continents and

the oceans. The continents and the oceans have various features which constitute the environment. Hence, Geography enables human beings to develop the basic skills that enable them to understand and interact with the environment in a sustainable way. Figure 1 illustrates the earth's surface.

## Meaning of the environment
On the earth's surface, human beings are surrounded by various things. These things and human beings are part of the environment. Therefore, the environment includes all the things that surround human beings. The things that surround human beings can be either natural or man-made. Natural things are those which are not created by human beings such as mountains, plants, animals, rivers, land and air. Man-made things include houses, roads, gardens, tables, cars and other things that are not natural. Information about the components that form the

### Our school environment

> [diagram: person writing at a desk] **Activity 1**
>
> Observe various things found in your school environment, then:
> 
> 1. Identify the natural and man-made things you have observed.
> 2. Explain the benefits of the things you have identified.

A school environment consists of all the things found at the school. These things are like classrooms, teachers' offices and houses, trees, flower gardens, play grounds, the national flag and toilets. Other things found in the school environment are dormitories, a library, laboratories and natural things such as rivers and hills. Inside the classroom there are desks, chairs, tables, books, a board and other teaching and learning materials. In addition, pupils, teachers and other staff are part of the school environment. The environment of one school may be different from or similar to that of another school, depending on the specific things found in a school. Basically, the things found in a school environment are important in teaching and learning processes; thus, they have to be cared for.

The home environment includes all the things found at home. These things can be inside or outside the house. ### Relationship between Geography and the Environment

Geography and the Environment are related concepts that are difficult to separate. We have learnt that the environment encompasses all the things that surround us, while Geography explains those things. Therefore,

--- PAGE 15 ---

<!--folio:8-->
Geography helps us to understand the environment that surrounds human beings and how to interact with them. Things like the forests, mountains, lakes, rivers, oceans, weather, land and animals around human beings constitute what we call is Geography and the Environment as a discipline. Therefore, it is important to study both at the same time.
