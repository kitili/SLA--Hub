### Subtracting numbers horizontally by regrouping

Numbers can be subtracted horizontally by regrouping tens into ones.

#### Example 1
$23 - 19 =$

**Solution**
[diagram: 23 - 19 = 4 with lines connecting 3 to 9 and 2 to 1]

**Steps**
1. Subtract the ones:  
   $3 - 9$, is not possible.

   [diagram: 23 - 19 = [ ] with arrow connecting 3 and 9 labeled "It is not possible"]

2. Take 1 ten from the group of 2 tens. Change it into 10 ones. Add the

<!--folio:106-->

ones: $10 + 3 = 13$. Remember 1 ten remained in the tens place.

[diagram: 23 - 19 = [ ] with 1 above 2 and 13 above 3]

3. Now subtract the ones:  
   $13 - 9 = 4$. Write 4 in the ones place of the answer.

   [diagram: 23 - 19 = [ 4 ] with 1 above 2, 13 above 3, and arrow connecting 13 and 9 to 4]

4. Subtract the tens:  
   $1 - 1 = 0$. Leave the tens place of the answer empty.

   [diagram: 23 - 19 = [ 4 ] with 1 above 2, 13 above 3, and arrow connecting 1 and 1]

   Therefore, $23 - 19 = 4$.
