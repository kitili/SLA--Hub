Data privacy policy

How we protect personal information about learners, families, and staff.

Key points

  1. Collect and share personal data only when you have a clear work reason.
  2. Do not discuss learner or staff private details in public spaces or group chats.
  3. Store files in approved systems — not on open USB drives or personal email.
  4. Photos and videos of learners need the right permissions before sharing.
  5. If data is exposed by mistake, report it immediately — silence makes it worse.
