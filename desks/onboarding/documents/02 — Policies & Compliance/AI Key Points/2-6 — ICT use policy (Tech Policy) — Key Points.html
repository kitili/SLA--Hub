ICT use policy (Tech Policy)

How we use school devices, accounts, and networks safely and professionally.

Key points

  1. School accounts and devices are for professional work — treat them as Silverleaf property.
  2. Protect passwords; never share login details with learners or colleagues.
  3. Do not install unapproved software or store sensitive data on personal devices.
  4. Online communication with learners and families must stay professional and recorded where required.
  5. Report lost devices, phishing, or suspected breaches immediately to IT.
