Child protection policy

Every adult is responsible for keeping children safe and reporting concerns quickly.

Key points

  1. Child protection is everyone's duty — not only teachers or safeguarding leads.
  2. Know the difference between a concern, a disclosure, and an emergency.
  3. Report promptly through the named safeguarding route; do not investigate alone.
  4. Never promise a child you will keep a safety concern secret.
  5. Maintain safe adult–child boundaries in person and online.
