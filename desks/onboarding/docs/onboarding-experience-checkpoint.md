# Onboarding Experience Checkpoint

## What is now done

- Login works locally for staff and admin flows after rebuilding the local PGlite database.
- Admins sign in with email and admin password instead of Staff ID.
- Admin link is hidden from non-admin users and visible to admins.
- Admins can change their admin password from the dashboard.
- The language switcher no longer says "Language:" in the navbar.
- The member experience now says "learning items" instead of feeling like a document folder.
- Old local documents are restored through the hub from the preserved `documents/` folder.
- PDFs, images, videos, YouTube links, and DOCX files open inside the hub viewer.
- The viewer includes a "Mark as done" action so staff can complete an item while reading.
- The bio profile includes a preparation checklist and support links for NSSF, TIN, and NHIF.
- Section pages now include guided orientation, outcomes, how-to-use notes, media notes, and reflection prompts.
- The Welcome section now has a story-led video layer with chapters and reflection prompts.
- Admin dashboard includes content readiness counts.
- E2E sign-in coverage exists for staff login with a local ed-admin mock.
