# Onboarding Experience Gap Plan

This product should feel like a guided Silverleaf onboarding journey, not a protected folder of files. The current app now adds section-level guidance, reflection prompts, and learning-item framing, but the remaining work is content production.

## Content Standard

Each section should have:

- A short orientation paragraph that explains why the section matters.
- A clear outcome: what the staff member should understand or be able to do.
- A small number of learning items with meaningful titles and notes.
- At least one human element where appropriate: leader video, audio welcome, scenario, or reflection prompt.
- A checkpoint that tests the essentials, not trivia.

## Video Work Required

- Split the current CEO welcome video into shorter clips mapped to specific sections.
- Add missing leader and head-of-department introductions.
- Add head-of-school introductions where school-level context matters.
- Prefer clips under 2-4 minutes with one clear purpose per clip.
- Avoid uploading a full unedited recording as a single onboarding item.

## Priority Gaps

- `welcome`: edited CEO welcome plus central team/leadership introductions.
- `support`: short clips from HR, IT/operations, and key central support contacts.
- `teaching`: Head of School and academic leadership orientation clips.
- `digital-tools`: short screen recordings for setup tasks.
- `safeguarding`: scenario-led explanation, not only policy reading.

## Definition Of 100%

- Staff can move through onboarding with context, not just file links.
- Every major section explains why it matters and what to take away.
- Videos are edited, purposeful, and placed in relevant sections.
- Admins can update content without engineering support.
- Progress monitoring shows who is active, behind, or complete.
- The experience is polished enough to show to external stakeholders.
