This document explains how to use the Silverleaf Onboarding and Hiring Portal
from beginning to end. It is written in three parts:

  Part A — HR Guide

  Part C — IT Guide

Read only the section(s) that apply to your role.

================================================================================
PART A — HR GUIDE
================================================================================

As HR, your job in this portal covers two main areas:
  1. Monitoring staff through their onboarding journey
  2. Managing job applicants through the hiring pipeline

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A1. LOGGING IN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to the portal URL provided by IT and log in with your staff credentials.
Once logged in, navigate to the Admin area from the top menu. You will land on
the admin home page, which gives you two entry points:

  - Onboarding Admin   → monitor staff progress, manage content
  - Hiring Portal      → manage job applicants

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A2. MONITORING STAFF ONBOARDING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Members

This is your main monitoring dashboard.

STAT CARDS AT THE TOP
─────────────────────
Four numbers are shown at the top of the page:

  - Total Staff            Total registered members in the system
  - Completed              Members who have finished all onboarding sections
  - Checkpoints Passed     Combined quiz passes across all members
  - At Risk                Members currently flagged as orange or red

RISK BADGES EXPLAINED
─────────────────────
Each staff member has a risk badge next to their name:

  GREEN   The member is progressing on schedule. No action needed.

  ORANGE  The member is falling behind. They have either:
          - Not logged in for 7 or more days, OR
          - Fallen more than 10 percentage points behind the expected
            pace (which is based on days since they started)
          ACTION: Follow up with the member directly and encourage them
          to continue.

  RED     The member is seriously off track. They have either:
          - Passed the 42-day deadline without completing onboarding, OR
          - Fallen more than 25 percentage points behind the expected pace
          ACTION: Immediate follow-up required. Contact the member and
          their line manager if needed.

Note: Risk is calculated based on quiz completions only, not document reads.
A member cannot reduce their risk simply by clicking through materials without
passing the checkpoint quizzes at the end of each section.

FILTERING BY CAMPUS
───────────────────
Use the campus filter dropdown at the top of the members table to view
only the staff from a specific campus (e.g. Usa River, Arusha).

VIEWING A MEMBER'S FULL PROFILE
────────────────────────────────
Click on any member's name to open their detail page. You will see:

  - Section-by-section progress (which items read, which checkpoints passed)
  - Policy declarations they have signed (with date and name)
  - Their Bio Data Form contents if they have submitted it:
      Personal details, contact information, ID numbers (National ID,
      NSSF, TIN, NHIF), employment details, bank account, spouse and
      children, family contacts, emergency contacts, relatives employed
      at Silverleaf, academic qualifications, employment history,
      professional references, and legal declarations.
  - Uploaded documents (CV, certificates) — you can view these in-browser
  - A PDF download button to generate their complete bio data profile

AUTOMATED DAILY EMAILS
───────────────────────
Every morning at 7:00 AM (EAT) the system automatically sends emails:

  - Every orange and red member receives a personal reminder asking them
    to log back in and continue their onboarding.
  - Every member who has COMPLETED onboarding but has NOT yet submitted
    the Bio Data Form receives a separate reminder to fill it in.
  - You (the HR email address) receive a single daily summary email
    listing all off-track members and all members missing a bio form,
    with their current progress and risk level.

You do not need to do anything to trigger these — they run automatically.
If you are not receiving the daily summary email, contact IT to confirm
that the correct HR email address is saved in Settings.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A3. MANAGING THE HIRING PIPELINE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Hiring

THE PIPELINE BOARD
──────────────────
Candidates are organised in a Kanban board with stages from left to right:

  New → Screening → Interview → Assessment → Offer → Hired / Rejected

Each card shows the candidate's name and the role they applied for.
Use the Advance button on a card to move the candidate to the next stage
without having to open their full profile.

Click on a candidate's name to open their full detail page, where you can:
  - View all their submitted information (from the application form or
    entered manually)
  - See their LinkedIn profile and CV link
  - Add internal notes
  - Change their stage or mark them as hired/rejected

ADDING CANDIDATES MANUALLY
───────────────────────────
If a candidate applied outside the Google Form (by email or in person):

  1. From the Hiring board, click "Add Candidate Manually"
  2. Fill in their name, email, role applied for, and any other details
  3. Click Save — they appear in the New column

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A4. IMPORTING FROM THE GOOGLE FORM (SHEET IMPORT)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Hiring → Import from Application Form

This page lets you bring candidates from the Google Form responses into
the hiring pipeline without re-entering their data.

STEP 1 — CHECK THE OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────
At the top of the page is the "Application form overview" card. It shows:
  - Total number of applications received in the sheet
  - A bar chart of applications grouped by position

Click on the card header to expand or collapse the chart.

STEP 2 — SEARCH FOR CANDIDATES
────────────────────────────────
Two search modes are available:

  By Position  Type a position name or keyword (e.g. "Mathematics" or
               "Head of ECE"). The search is partial — you do not need
               to type the full title. All matching applications will
               appear in the results table below.

  By Email     Type the candidate's exact email address to find a single
               specific applicant.

Click Search after entering your query.

STEP 3 — REVIEW AND IMPORT
────────────────────────────
The results table shows matching candidates with their name, email,
role applied for, location, and import status:

  New         Not yet in the system — you can import this candidate
  In system   Already imported — the row is greyed out and cannot be
              selected again

To import:
  1. Tick the checkboxes next to the candidates you want to import
     (or click "Select all" to select every new candidate at once)
  2. Click "Import X into system"
  3. A green confirmation banner will confirm how many were imported

After import, the candidates appear in the Hiring board under "New".

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A5. PERFORMANCE TASKS (OPTIONAL ASSESSMENT STEP)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Hiring → Performance Tasks

This area lets you manage assignments that are sent to shortlisted
candidates as part of the selection process. Details are managed
from this page and linked to individual candidate profiles.

PART B — ADMIN GUIDE
================================================================================

As an admin, you manage the content that staff members work through
during onboarding — sections, learning materials, and quizzes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B1. UNDERSTANDING THE STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The onboarding portal is organised as follows:

  Sections       The top-level chapters (e.g. Welcome, HR Policies,
                 Child Protection). Staff see all sections from day one.

  Items          The individual learning tasks within a section
                 (e.g. a document to read, a video to watch, a policy
                 to sign). Each item can have one or more materials.

  Materials      The actual files — PDFs, videos, YouTube links — that
                 are attached to an item. One item can have materials
                 in multiple languages.

  Checkpoints    A quiz at the end of each section. Staff must pass the
                 quiz before the section is counted as complete.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B2. MANAGING SECTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Sections

THE SECTIONS LIST
─────────────────
You will see all sections in the order they appear to staff. Each row shows
the icon, title (English and Swahili), number of items, whether a quiz exists,
and whether the section is published.

  - Published    Visible to staff
  - Draft        Hidden from staff — use this while building new content

Reorder sections by dragging rows or using the up/down controls.
Toggle published/draft status directly from this list.

CREATING A NEW SECTION
───────────────────────
  1. Click "New section" at the top of the page
  2. Enter: icon (emoji), English title, Swahili title, English description,
     Swahili description
  3. Save — the section appears in the list as a draft
  4. Set it to Published when content is ready

EDITING A SECTION
─────────────────
Click on a section name to open its editor. From there you can:

  - Update the title, description, or icon
  - View and manage the section's items (listed in a table)
  - Add new items using the "Add item" panel on the same page
  - Access the quiz manager for that section via the link at the top

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B3. MANAGING ITEMS WITHIN A SECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

From the Section Editor (Admin → Sections → [Section Name]):

ADDING AN ITEM
──────────────
  1. Click "Add item"
  2. Enter the item title (English and Swahili) and select the item type:
       - Document     A PDF or Office file to read
       - Video        A video file or YouTube embed
       - Declaration  A policy the member must sign before proceeding
       - Key Points   An AI-generated summary page
  3. Save the item — it now appears in the section's item list

ATTACHING A MATERIAL TO AN ITEM
─────────────────────────────────
Each item needs at least one material (the actual file or link).

  1. From the section editor, click the Update action on the item
  2. If the material is a file (PDF, video): click Choose File, select
     the file, optionally preview it, then click Upload
  3. If the material is a YouTube video: click "Edit link", paste the
     YouTube URL (any format — watch, short, or embed link all work),
     then save

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B4. MANAGING ALL MATERIALS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Materials

This page gives you a full list of every file and YouTube link across
all sections in one place. Useful for auditing what has been uploaded
and replacing outdated files.

Each row shows:
  - Filename (or "YouTube: [video-id]" for YouTube materials)
  - Which section item it belongs to
  - Language
  - File size
  - Upload date

UPDATING A DOCUMENT
────────────────────
  1. Click "Update" on the material row
  2. A panel appears below the row:
       a. Click "Choose File" and select the replacement file
       b. Optionally click "Preview" to check the file before uploading
       c. Click "Upload" to replace — the old file is removed automatically

EDITING A YOUTUBE LINK
───────────────────────
  1. Click "Edit link" on a YouTube material row
  2. Paste the new YouTube URL into the input field
  3. Click Save

DELETING A MATERIAL
────────────────────
Click the delete icon on a material row. You will be asked to confirm.
Deleting a material does not delete the item — the item will simply
have no file until a new one is uploaded.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B5. MANAGING QUIZZES (CHECKPOINTS)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Each section can have one checkpoint quiz that staff must pass to
complete the section.

Go to: Admin → Sections → [Section Name] → Manage Quiz

From here you can:
  - Add questions (multiple choice or short answer)
  - Set the passing score threshold
  - Edit or delete existing questions

Staff see the quiz questions without the correct answers shown. Their
answers are submitted and scored automatically. Results are recorded and
shown in the member's progress profile.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B6. MANAGING CAMPUSES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Campuses

Add or edit campus names here. Campus names are used to:
  - Categorise staff members
  - Filter the Members monitoring table
  - Label members in HR report emails

Add a campus by clicking "New campus" and entering the campus name.

PART C — IT GUIDE
================================================================================

As IT, your responsibilities cover system configuration, onboarding new
staff into the portal, and troubleshooting access and content issues.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C1. SYSTEM SETTINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Settings

CONFIGURING THE HR EMAIL ADDRESS
─────────────────────────────────
The daily summary email is sent to the address stored under "hr_email".

  1. Locate the "HR Email" setting
  2. Enter or update the email address
  3. Save

If this is left blank, the system defaults to jobs@silverleaf.co.tz.

CONFIGURING TEAM INTRO VIDEOS
──────────────────────────────
The Welcome section shows video intro cards for key staff members
(CEO, Head of School, HR Lead, and Department Leads). Each can have
a YouTube embed link.

  For single roles (CEO, Head of School, HR):
    - Find the corresponding setting
    - Paste the YouTube video URL
    - Save

  For Department Leads (multiple entries):
    - Each entry has a label (e.g. "Head of Mathematics"), a YouTube URL,
      and an active toggle
    - Add, edit, or deactivate entries as staff change roles

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C2. REGISTERING A NEW STAFF MEMBER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Staff members self-register using the link provided to them. Once they
create an account, they appear in the Members list automatically.

After registration:
  1. Go to Admin → Members and find the new member
  2. Assign them to the correct campus using the campus dropdown on
     their profile page
  3. If they need admin access, toggle the "Admin" switch on their profile

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C3. RESETTING THE ADMIN PASSWORD
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Members

Scroll to the bottom of the page where the admin password form is located.
Enter and confirm the new admin password, then save.

This password is separate from individual staff login credentials. It is
the master password used for admin-level authentication.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C4. DAILY CRON — HOW IT WORKS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The system automatically sends reminder emails every day at 7:00 AM (EAT).
This is handled by Vercel Cron and does not require any manual action.

The cron job is protected by a secret token (CRON_SECRET) set in the
Vercel environment variables. Do not expose this token publicly.

To verify the cron is running:
  1. Log in to the Vercel dashboard
  2. Go to the project → Settings → Cron Jobs
  3. Confirm the offtrack-reminder job is active and shows a recent
     last-run timestamp

If the cron is not running, check:
  - That CRON_SECRET is set in the Vercel environment variables
  - That GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON is set (required for Google Sheets)
  - That the SMTP/email credentials (used by sendHiringEmail) are valid

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C5. ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES (REQUIRED)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The following environment variables must be set in Vercel for the system
to function correctly:

  DATABASE_URL                   PostgreSQL connection string
  NEXTAUTH_SECRET                Auth session secret
  NEXTAUTH_URL                   Full URL of the deployed portal
  CRON_SECRET                    Vercel cron protection token
  GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON    Service account credentials for Sheets
  GOOGLE_SHEETS_HIRING_FORM_ID   The Google Sheet ID for hiring forms
  GOOGLE_HR_EMAIL                Fallback HR email (if not set in Settings)
  BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN          Vercel Blob storage token (for file uploads)

Contact the Vercel project owner if any of these are missing or need
to be rotated.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C6. DIAGNOSING GOOGLE SHEET ISSUES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

If HR reports that candidates from the Google Form are not appearing
in search results, use the built-in diagnostic endpoint:

  GET /api/admin/hiring/sheet-debug    (admin login required)

This returns:
  - The header row of the sheet (to verify column order)
  - Total number of rows
  - Unique values found in each column in the role-name area (columns
    10–16), so you can identify column mapping issues
  - A count of rows where email or name is blank (these are silently
    skipped during import)

The most common cause of missing candidates is inconsistent role name
formatting in the sheet (e.g. two slightly different titles for the
same position). This is a data entry issue in the Google Form or sheet,
not a system bug.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
C7. CONTENT READINESS CHECK
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Go to: Admin → Members

Below the stat cards, a content readiness panel shows:
  - How many materials (files/YouTube links) are uploaded
  - How many section items still have NO material attached
  - How many YouTube embeds are registered

Use this panel to confirm that all sections have content before a new
intake of staff begins their onboarding.
