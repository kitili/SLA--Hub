No cash policy

Silverleaf does not allow staff to collect, hold, or move cash for school business.

Key points

  1. Do not accept cash from parents, vendors, or colleagues for school fees or activities.
  2. Direct all payments through approved official channels only.
  3. If someone offers cash, politely refuse and explain the no-cash rule.
  4. Report pressure to handle cash to your manager or finance contact immediately.
  5. This policy protects you and the school from loss, disputes, and misuse.
